Opket Child Safety Standards

Last updated: April 14, 2026

Opket has a zero-tolerance policy for child sexual abuse and exploitation, including child sexual abuse material (CSAM/CSAE), grooming, sexual extortion, trafficking, and any content or behavior that sexualizes or exploits minors.

Zero tolerance for CSAE Public reporting contact Account and content enforcement Law enforcement escalation when required

Scope

These standards apply to all public and private use of Opket, including messages, shared media, profile content, room activity, live communication features, links, and any user-generated content transmitted through the service.

Prohibited Content And Conduct

  • Any child sexual abuse material, child nudity intended for sexual purposes, or exploitative child imagery
  • Grooming, solicitation, coercion, or manipulation of a minor for sexual purposes
  • Sexual conversations with minors or attempts to obtain sexual images from minors
  • Threats, blackmail, sextortion, trafficking, or attempts to exploit minors on or through the platform
  • Sharing links, instructions, or communities intended to distribute or facilitate CSAE content
  • Impersonation of minors or age misrepresentation for exploitative purposes

Enforcement

  • Opket may remove violating content without notice
  • Opket may suspend or permanently block access for users involved in abusive behavior
  • Opket may preserve relevant records when legally required or reasonably necessary for safety review
  • Opket may report credible CSAE activity to appropriate authorities or reporting organizations when required by law or platform policy

Reporting

If you believe any content, behavior, or user activity involves child exploitation, child sexual abuse, grooming, or immediate risk to a minor, contact Opket immediately:

Email: atlamfik@gmail.com

Include the room code, usernames, screenshots, timestamps, and a short description when possible. If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or law enforcement first.

Response Commitment

  • Reports are prioritized for urgent review
  • Emergency child-safety reports should be sent by email immediately
  • Content or account restrictions may be applied while a report is being reviewed

Children's Access

Opket is not intended to be used for sexual or exploitative interactions of any kind, and no user may use the service to target or endanger minors. Any attempt to use the service in this way is a severe violation of these standards.
